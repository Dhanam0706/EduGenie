## Define Problem Statements (Customer Problem Statement Template)

EduGenie helps students who need to understand, revise and test a topic quickly, but find textbooks too dense and general search results too scattered or too long.

| Problem Statement (PS) | I am (Customer) | I'm trying to | But | Because | Which makes me feel |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| PS-1 | A school or college student meeting a hard concept for the first time | Understand it in simple words with an example | Textbooks and videos assume background I do not have | Explanations are written for experts, not beginners | Confused and discouraged |
| PS-2 | A student with long class notes before an exam | Revise the important points quickly | Reading and shortening the notes takes hours | There is no quick way to pick out the key points from my own material | Stressed about time |
| PS-3 | A student who wants to check how well I understand a topic | Practise with questions on exactly that topic | Question banks do not cover my notes or my exact topic | Writing good multiple-choice questions myself is slow | Unsure of my level |
| PS-4 | A self-learner starting a new subject such as SQL | Know what to learn first and what comes later | I do not know the order of topics or good resources | Search results are scattered and not organised by level | Overwhelmed |
